01. About Me

I’m Shivam, a developer who enjoys understanding how things work and turning that understanding into useful software.

My interests sit at the intersection of software engineering, algorithms, and practical problem solving. I enjoy building functional, well-structured digital experiences and tackling technical challenges with patience, curiosity, and attention to detail.

I learn best by building. Each project gives me a chance to explore a new idea, test my assumptions, and improve the way I think about performance, usability, and design. Featured Project

Ultrasonic Car Backup Sensor

A real-time parking safety system that uses an ultrasonic distance sensor to measure nearby objects. As an obstacle gets closer, the warning beeps become faster and more LEDs illuminate to communicate distance clearly.

Designed and built independently, including the circuit board, wiring, hardware integration, and every line of C++ control logic. The system reads distances to under 3 centimeters with no noticeable lag and earned a 100% final grade in high school tech.
